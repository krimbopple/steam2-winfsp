TeraRelease Steam2 extractor depot-key table
---------------------------------------------
The Steam2 depot key table in src/depot_keys.cpp is derived from the extractor
source distributed with TeraRelease:
http://us.steam2.download/extractor/src.zip

The upstream archive did not state a separate license for this table. It is
redistributed here with attribution to the TeraRelease extractor authors and
contributors. The table is functional data used to decrypt user-supplied
Steam2 depot archives. Valve archive payloads are not distributed by this
project.
